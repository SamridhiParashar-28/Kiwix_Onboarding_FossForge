Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR)

ZIM Archive: wikimed_en.zim • Verified Offline Copy

Cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) is an emergency procedure that combines chest compressions often with artificial ventilation in an effort to manually preserve intact brain function until further measures are taken to restore spontaneous blood circulation and breathing in a person who is in cardiac arrest.

Key Offline Protocol:

  • Rate: 100 to 120 chest compressions per minute.
  • Depth: At least 2 inches (5 cm) but not more than 2.4 inches (6 cm).
  • Ratio: 30 compressions followed by 2 rescue breaths for adults.
